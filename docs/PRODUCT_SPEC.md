# Signal Product Specification

## Core promise

Signal finds commercially meaningful changes and opportunities across a company's external footprint, explains the evidence, recommends what to do next, and records the outcome.

## Core pipeline

Company → Research → Evidence → Fusion → Contradiction check → Confidence → Commercial score → Decision → Action → Outcome.

## Decision states

- Opportunity: sufficiently strong evidence and commercial relevance.
- Review: potentially valuable but evidence is weak, stale or conflicting.
- Ignore: insufficient commercial value or evidence.

## SME strategy

Small businesses may have less structured data. Signal should broaden research across websites, directories, jobs, local information, industry sources and observable digital gaps. A digital/automation opportunity must be labelled as a hypothesis unless evidence supports a stronger conclusion.

## Customer data

Customers may import existing customer lists and connect CRM systems. Existing relationships should influence prioritisation without overwriting source evidence.

## Learning

Outcomes such as meetings, qualified opportunities, wins, nurture, no response, lost and not-a-fit are stored for measurement. Learning must be controlled and evidence-based rather than allowing the model to self-modify blindly.

## Product tiers

Tier limits will be enforced by usage, research depth, monitoring frequency, seats, CRM integrations and workflow capabilities. Exact pricing remains a commercial decision after cost testing.
